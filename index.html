四

The Party, from branch to Politburo中国共产党的组织体系

The Communist Party of China is a mass party of roughly one hundred million members organized into more than five million grassroots units. Power inside it runs on democratic centralism: each level is elected from below and bound by the decisions of the level above. Diagram 2 at the top of the page shows the chain from the branch to the General Secretary.

五

Elections and the five levels of people's congresses人民代表大会制度与选举

The people's congress system is the fundamental political system of the state. Deputies at the two lowest levels are elected directly by all voters; each higher congress is elected by the congress below it. Every state organ at every level is then created by, answers to and can be removed by the congress of that level.
